# Governance Model

The governance model defines who can approve budget increases, launch new
campaign types, and change account-level defaults.

## Budget approval chain

Any budget increase above a campaign's original cap must be approved by the
requesting team's budget owner. Increases above $50,000/month for a single
campaign additionally require sign-off from the Finance Governance Council,
which meets weekly.

## New campaign types

Enabling a new campaign type for an account requires approval from the
account's platform administrator. See the roles doc for who holds that
role on a given account.

## Auditing

Every approval action is logged with the approver's identity, the amount or
change approved, and a timestamp. Audit logs are retained for seven years.
