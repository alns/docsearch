# Governance Roles

## Budget owner

Assigned per team. Can approve budget increases up to $50,000/month for any
campaign owned by their team.

## Platform administrator

Assigned per account. Can enable new campaign types, manage account-level
defaults, and add or remove budget owners.

## Finance Governance Council

A standing committee that reviews and approves any single-campaign budget
increase above $50,000/month, regardless of team.
