# Governance Roles

Operational reference for who holds which governance role and how to
request a change. For the policy reasoning behind why these approvals
exist, see the strategy team's budget governance doc — this page is about
who and how, not why.

## Budget owner

Assigned per team, by the team's manager, in the admin console under
Team Settings → Roles. Can approve budget increases up to $50,000/month for
any campaign owned by their team.

To find your team's current budget owner, check the admin console or ask
in #team-ops.

## Platform administrator

Assigned per account by an existing platform administrator. Can enable new
campaign types, manage account-level defaults, and add or remove budget
owners.

## Finance Governance Council

A standing committee that reviews and approves any single-campaign budget
increase above $50,000/month, regardless of team. Meets weekly; to request
a slot, file a request in the #finance-governance channel at least three
business days ahead of the meeting you want.
