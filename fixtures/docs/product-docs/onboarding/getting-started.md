# Getting Started

Welcome. This guide walks through creating your first account, inviting
teammates, and connecting a billing method.

## Steps

1. Create an account and verify the workspace email domain.
2. Invite teammates and assign each one a role.
3. Connect a billing method before creating your first campaign — campaigns
   cannot enter the active state without one.
