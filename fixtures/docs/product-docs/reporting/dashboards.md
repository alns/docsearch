# Reporting Dashboards

Dashboards summarize spend, reach, and conversions across campaigns.

## Standard dashboards

- **Spend summary** — daily and cumulative spend against budget cap.
- **Performance summary** — impressions, clicks, and conversions by
  campaign.
- **Audience summary** — reach broken down by the targeting dimensions in
  use.

## Export

Any dashboard can be exported as CSV or scheduled as a recurring email
report.
