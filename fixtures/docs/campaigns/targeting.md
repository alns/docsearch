# Campaign Targeting

Targeting determines which audience segments a campaign's ad sets are
eligible to reach.

## Targeting dimensions

- **Geography** — country, region, or a custom radius around a point.
- **Demographics** — age range and language.
- **Interests** — derived from an account's first-party audience data.
- **Custom audiences** — uploaded lists or lookalikes built from them.

## Defaults

New ad sets inherit the account's default targeting template unless
overridden. Default targeting templates are managed separately from budget
and governance settings.
